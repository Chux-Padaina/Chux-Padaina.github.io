Contribution Tracker - Unreal Plugin

UE5 editor plugin for contribution metadata, provenance, and project summaries.

A UE5 editor tool piloted and built for my master’s thesis study, focused on tracking contribution history across student game projects without forcing teams into a heavy documentation process. It attaches metadata to level actors, stores a shared per-level project record, tracks editor events, highlights missing or pending contribution information, and supports batch review from a central control panel. It also captures broader work like testing, organization, and Content Drawer changes, then turns that history into readable summaries for review and portfolio use.

Forensics Sim for Law Enforcement Training

Unity serious game for guided crime-scene training and procedural learning.

A browser-playable Unity training simulation for forensic investigation workflows, developed with law-enforcement and forensic-science input. The game models a guided crime-scene scenario where trainees inspect evidence, gather fingerprints, document observations, consult in-game reference material, and progress through procedure-driven objectives. It is currently being used in law-enforcement training in Greece, and our related paper is getting published in EDULEARN 2026.
